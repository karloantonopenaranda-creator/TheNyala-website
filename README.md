TheNyala is a tarot reading and birth-chart (astrology) business. This website introduces the brand, presents the readings on offer, and lets a visitor book one by filling in an intake form. Every reading is one-to-one and prepared in advance from the visitor's intake, then delivered either as a written reading (PDF) with an audio recording, or live by video call.

This document covers everything about the site as it stands today: what each page does, how its features work, the design concept behind it, the files involved, and what still needs doing before it can go live.

## The site at a glance

The site has **three pages**: the scrollable home page, the booking intake, and a draft reviews page.

| Page | File | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Home page**, first screen | `index.html` | First impression: Nyala, the brand spirit, on the business's rust paper inside its embossed frame, with the headline and a "Find Your Path" button. |
| **Home page**, scrolling down: Readings and Offerings | `index.html#readings` | The three readings on the business's own cream cards, each with its price and a "Choose this reading" button. |
| **Reviews (draft)** | `reviews.html` | A styled page with three clearly marked review placeholders; no testimonials have been supplied yet. |
| **Begin Your Reading** | `book.html` | The intake form, on a focused page of its own. It shows only the questions the chosen reading needs, checks the answers and confirms them. |

**Why the mix:** looking at the readings is relaxed browsing, so it flows on one page from the first screen. Filling in the intake is a task, so it gets a quiet page with nothing else on it, and its scrolling form box never competes with a scrolling page.

**How a visitor moves through it:**

```
Home: first screen ──"Find Your Path" (glides down)──▶ Readings and Offerings ──"Choose this reading"──▶ book.html ──"Send my intake"──▶ Thank you
                  └──"Readings" in the menu──┘
```

**The offering**, as on the business's own service cards:

| Card | How it's delivered | Intake asks for |
|---|---|---|
| **Digital Tarot Reading** | One question or three questions, as a PDF with an audio recording; or a live 30-minute session by video call | Question formats: current situation, the question(s) and anything to focus on. Session: questions, ranked areas to explore and anything else. Every format: a preferred date. |
| **Birth Chart Reading** | A PDF with an audio recording | Date, exact time and place of birth, how sure they are of the time (1–10), main reason for booking, ranked areas to explore, anything else. |
| **Birth Chart + Tarot Reading** | A live session of 60 to 70 minutes by video call | Everything the birth chart needs, plus current situation, questions, ranked areas for the cards, anything to focus on and a preferred date. |

The tarot card says "PDF & audio recording, or live 30-minute session". The site assumes the one- and three-question formats are the PDF and audio version, and the session is the live one. **Confirm this with the business.**

Every reading also asks for a preferred name and an email address. Pronouns (optional) are asked for every reading except the Birth Chart Reading, following the business's intake spec.

Prices aren't decided yet, so every card shows "Price coming soon".

## Running the site

The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is no build step and nothing to install.

### Quickest way

Double-click `index.html`. It opens in your browser and works.

### Recommended: through the local server

Running the site through a small local web server is closer to how it will behave online. A ready-made server script uses the web server built into Windows, so it needs no installs.

1. Open a terminal in the `TheNyala` folder.
2. Run:
   ```
   powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\serve.ps1
   ```
3. Visit **http://localhost:8080/**.
4. Press **Ctrl+C** in the terminal to stop it.

Add `-Port 9000` (or another number) to the command if port 8080 is already in use.

After changing a file, press **Ctrl+F5** in the browser to make sure it loads the newest version.

## The pages

### 1. Home page (`index.html`)

One scrollable page in two sections, on the business's own rust-red paper. A plain patch of the paper, repeated, stays fixed behind the page while the sections scroll over it.

#### The first screen

A full-screen scene.

- **Background:** on wide screens (at least 1280px wide, 700px high, and at least 3:2), the business's whole background design: the rust paper with its embossed ornamental frame and the rounded label at the top left. The frame's decorated sides keep their exact shape on any screen, and only the plain middle stretches to fit the width (see [The paper and its frame](#the-paper-and-its-frame)). On narrower or taller screens, including phones, the frame wouldn't fit, so the plain paper shows instead.
- **Nyala:** the brand spirit, shown as a circular Vitruvian motif with a gentle float in cream linework, centered above the headline (see [How Nyala is placed](#how-nyala-is-placed)). Eleven embossed cards form a scattered background toward the outer areas on desktop; six smaller cards remain on phones and tablets.
- **Wordmark:** "TheNyala" in the brand's hand-drawn font, centred in the paper's rounded label (at the top left on screens without the frame).
- **Menu:** simple Anaktoria text links, level with the label and inside the paper's frame: **Readings**, **Daily Card** (still a placeholder), and **Reviews**. Readings and Reviews remain visible on phones. The menu scrolls away with the hero.
- **Centered introduction:** beneath Nyala, the headline "The stars remember / what you've forgotten." retains the hand-drawn font. Anaktoria copy reads: "Personal tarot and birth-chart readings to help you understand your patterns and choose your next step." The original framed **Find Your Path** button follows directly beneath it and links to the readings. Detailed delivery formats remain in the offerings section.

#### Readings and Offerings (scrolling down, `#readings`)

A pricing table on the plain paper: three columns, one per reading (stacked on phones), laid out like a classic pricing page (redesigned 2026-11-18 at the client's request, from a reference layout).

- **Heading:** "Readings and Offerings" in the hand-drawn font, with a short line underneath explaining that every reading is one-to-one, prepared from what the visitor shares when booking, and comes as a PDF with an audio recording or as a live video call.
- **Each column** is a leaf of aged parchment with a ragged edge and an inked double frame, in the antique, Vitruvian style of the opening collage, with a faint embossed tarot card pressed into it:
  1. a Roman numeral and the reading's name in rust lettering
  2. the engraving from that reading's card (a caterpillar, a chrysalis, a butterfly: a small metamorphosis from left to right), drawn over Vitruvian circle, square and triangle lines, cut out by `tools/build-engravings.ps1` into `assets/engravings/`
  3. who the reading is for, the price ("Price coming soon" for now), and its format (PDF and audio, live, or either)
  4. a full-width framed **"Choose this reading"** button, which opens the booking page with that reading already selected
  5. "What's included": a ticked list drawn from the booking form. Check its wording with the reader.
- The three parts (header, top, list) line up across the columns whatever their length.
- No column is highlighted as "most popular" (a business decision). There's no billing switch: readings have no billing periods.

### 2. Begin Your Reading (`book.html`)

The intake form, on a focused page of its own, laid out like a classic sign-up page: the plain rust paper fills the whole page, the glade sits on it at the left, and the form is a bordered box laid on it at the right. There's no menu; the wordmark links home.

- **Left side, the glade:** directly on the paper, with the wordmark at the top, Nyala floating in the middle and "Begin Your Reading" in the brand font at the bottom. The line under the heading describes the chosen reading, in the same words as its card. On desktop the page is exactly one screen tall, with the same margin above and below the box, so this side never moves. On tablets and phones it sits above the form box.
- **Right side, the intake:** a deep rust-brown box with a thin cream border and a soft shadow, laid on the paper, headed "Your intake", with "Not sure which reading? Compare them" linking back to the readings on the home page. On desktop the box is as tall as the glade and the form scrolls inside it, with a thin, faint scrollbar. On tablets and phones the box grows with the form and the page scrolls normally.
- **"Your reading":** three choice buttons. Arriving from a card on the home page selects the right one automatically. Arriving any other way, three numbered steps explain what happens next (choose a reading, share a little about yourself, your reader emails you), until the visitor picks one.
- **The questions:** only the sections the chosen reading needs appear:
  - **About you:** preferred name, pronouns (optional, where the spec asks for them), email
  - **Your birth chart** (Birth Chart Reading, and Birth Chart + Tarot Reading): date of birth, exact birth time, birthplace, a 1–10 slider for how sure they are of the time ("A rough guess" to "From my birth certificate"), main reason for booking, ranked areas to explore
  - **Your tarot reading:** a switch for **One question**, **Three questions** or **Live 30-minute session**, with a note that the question formats come as a PDF with an audio recording and the session is live by video call, then the questions that format needs
  - **Your reading date** (every reading with tarot in it, so not the Birth Chart Reading on its own): a month calendar for picking a preferred date, with the chosen day shown large beside it and a note that the reader confirms it by email
  - **Anything else you'd like your reader to know** (optional, where the spec includes it)
- **"Send my intake"** (a full-width, cream-filled button): checks the answers. If anything is missing or mistyped, each problem gets a plain message under its field, a count appears under the button, and the cursor moves to the first problem. Once everything is complete, the form is replaced by a thank-you panel naming the reading and the visitor and saying what happens next (for the live readings, the reader confirms a time for the video call; for the others, when the PDF and audio recording will be ready), with a "Back to the home page" button.

> **Important:** the form does not send the answers anywhere yet. It shows the thank-you, but nothing reaches the business. See [What's left before launch](#whats-left-before-launch).

## How the features work

The layout uses CSS; JavaScript handles the booking form and the home-page artwork's subtle cursor response:

| File | Used for | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `css/style.css` | Home page | Sizes and places the Vitruvian motif and decorative tarot artwork |
| `js/hero-motion.js` | Home-page Nyala | Automatic 6-second float and subtle mouse tilt | Gentler automatic 8-second float, no tilt |
| `js/main.js` | Retained legacy file, not loaded | Previous narrow-figure sizing logic |
| `js/booking.js` | Booking page | Runs the intake form |

### The paper and its frame

- **The plain paper:** a patch of the business's paper from inside its frame (`assets/paper.jpg`, 860×700) is repeated behind every page. The paper is lit so evenly that the copies meet without a visible join.
- **The framed first screen:** the business's whole design (`assets/paper-frame.jpg`) is stretched across the first screen in a special way. The left and right parts, which hold the label, the corner scrolls and the twisted side vines, are scaled to the screen's height and keep their exact shape. Only the plain middle, which holds just the straight top and bottom lines and paper, stretches sideways. So the frame fits any wide screen without its ornaments looking squashed or stretched.
- **Placing things on it:** the wordmark and menu use the frame's scale. The artwork, copy, and button follow normal document flow in the clear middle of the frame, with room above and below them.
- **When it shows:** screens at least 1280px wide, 700px high, and at least 3:2. Otherwise the plain paper shows and the wordmark and menu sit at the top corners.

The centered hero column is up to 620px wide, with a 28–40px headline and 20px introduction on desktop. Phones use a 24–34px headline and 19px introduction. All font families and artwork files are unchanged.

### How Nyala is placed

**Sizing.** CSS isolates the 864px circular motif in the supplied 1920×1080 PNG and centers it above the copy. Desktop diameter is min(38svh, 42vw, 480px). The original PNG stays intact, with the existing CSS filter rendering the linework in cream.

**Tablets and phones.** At 900px and below the motif is min(78vw, 40svh, 380px), with 104px top padding and 24px page margins. Copy begins 28px below the circle; the button follows 22px after the copy. The page scrolls naturally on short screens. At 1100px and below, six smaller embossed illustrations remain as a subtle background.

**Motion.** Nyala floats automatically over six seconds: up to 12px on desktop and 8px on smaller screens, with a subtle mouse tilt of at most 2 degrees. Reduced-motion settings use a gentler eight-second float (6px desktop, 4px on smaller screens) without cursor tilt. Motion pauses outside the viewport and in hidden tabs. There is no animation button, and old saved motion choices are ignored. Touch scrolling remains unaffected. Embossed symbols and the colored card fan stay static.

### The uploaded tarot artwork

Matched to the supplied subtle reference, the embossed field uses 60% opacity on desktop and 50% on smaller screens. The embossed cards form a background layer using their natural relief colors and transparency. The user approved overlaps with other artwork. No added visibility filters or bright outlines are used. The terracotta paper remains.

The original Magician and High Priestess pair is joined by nine more cards, with varied positions, sizes, and rotations. The background is clipped at the hero edges and stays behind the cream motif, text, and controls. At 1100px and below it uses six smaller illustrations at 50% group opacity. The colored fan above the service cards contains The Fool, The High Priestess, and Wheel of Fortune, in their original colors. On desktop it sits to the right of the readings introduction; at 900px and below it sits underneath, followed by the centered service-card column. It is decorative and does not draw cards or change the selected reading.

Fourteen optimized WebP files in `assets/deck/` include three colored cards and eleven embossed cards. Each has a JSON provenance note. Rebuild them with `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/build-deck-assets.ps1` (requires ffmpeg). The script resizes and encodes images only; it preserves their colors, proportions, and transparency. Source mappings are in `assets/deck/README.md`.

### The booking form

- **Fields per reading:** every section and field is tagged with the readings it belongs to. When a reading (or tarot format) is chosen, everything else is hidden and switched off, so hidden fields are never checked or sent.
- **Preselecting a reading:** the cards on the home page link to `book.html?reading=tarot`, `?reading=chart` or `?reading=combo`. The older `?reading=session` still works and opens the tarot session format.
- **Ranked areas to explore:** visitors tap topics in order of importance, and each gets a numbered badge (1, 2, 3…). Tapping one again removes it and renumbers the rest. The ranking is sent as text, for example "1. Money; 2. Love and relationships".
- **Checking answers:**
  - **Timing:** messages only appear after the first attempt to send, then update live as the visitor types.
  - **Wording:** each message says what's missing and how to fix it, for example: "Please enter your birth time. If you're not sure, give your best guess and set the confidence low."
  - **What's checked:** the email address is checked for a sensible format, and the date of birth can't be in the future.
- **Preferred date:** a month calendar, starting on the visitor's usual first day of the week (from their browser's region). The earliest date is tomorrow, since the reader prepares from the intake first, and the latest is six months ahead. Open days show as soft tiles; past days, today and anything outside that range are dim and can't be chosen, and today has a small dot. The chosen day fills with moonlight and is written out large beside the calendar (the day number, then "Thursday" and "November 2026"; under the calendar on phones), and the thank-you mentions it. It works by keyboard too: arrow keys move by day and week, Page Up and Page Down by month, Home and End to the ends of the week. It's sent as `preferred_date`, for example `2026-11-12`, ready for the GoHighLevel calendar later. To change the earliest day or the six-month limit, edit the `min` and `max` lines in the preferred-date part of `js/booking.js`.
- **What gets sent:** the reading's name, the tarot format (for the Digital Tarot Reading, for example "One question (PDF and audio)" or "Live 30-minute session"), the preferred date (for readings with tarot), and every answer that applies. For now, the form only prints this to the browser's developer console; see the note in `js/booking.js`.

## The design concept

### Creative direction: "Nyala on the Paper"

The site is a piece of old stationery: the business's own rust-red paper, flecked and lightly worn, with an embossed ornamental frame on the first screen and a rounded label holding the wordmark. The Vitruvian Nyala motif is its cream-lined centerpiece, with a scattered, overlapping embossed tarot background. Colored tarot illustrations introduce the readings; cream lace service cards, scrolled button frames, and cream text keep the interface cohesive.

The feeling is **quiet, keepsake-like and storybook**: calm and guiding rather than pushy or fear-based. That matches how the business describes itself: honest guidance, no invented claims and no predictions.

**Principles:**
- **One focal point per screen.** On the first screen it's Nyala; nothing else competes.
- **The artwork carries the colour.** The paper and original colored illustrations provide the color. Text and buttons stay cream, with deep rust-brown fills where needed.
- **Keep the artwork whole.** The paper's frame, the service cards and the button frames are shown as the business made them, never redrawn or recoloured, and nothing crosses the frame's ornaments, Nyala or the text.
- **Motion is gentle.** The home-page Nyala drifts and responds subtly to the mouse; its background artwork stays static. The booking figure floats slowly. Service cards lift slightly on hover.

### Colour

| Role | Colour | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#8f3f20` (and the paper texture itself) | The background of every page |
| Cream | `#f6f0e6` | All interface text, borders and buttons, matching the service cards and button frames |
| Muted cream | cream at about 60–90% | Secondary text |
| Deep rust-brown | about `#300e08` at 40–84% | The booking box, the form's fields and choices, and the fill inside the button frames |
| Ink | `#2a0c07` | Dark text on cream, such as the "Send my intake" label |
| Warm shadow | near-black brown | Text and drop shadows |
| Error coral | `#f0a596` / `#f4b3a6` | Form error borders and messages, and nothing else |

### Typography

| Font | Used for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **The Nyala Font** (the business's own hand-drawn font) | The "TheNyala" wordmark, page headings, the hero headline, and the thank-you heading | A monoline script with little spiral curls at the ends of the strokes. It gives the brand its storybook voice. |
| **Anaktoria** (secondary font, by George Douros) | Titles one level below the headings: the prices under the cards, "Your intake" and the form's section titles, the intro lines under the headlines, and the big date number in the calendar. Also the labels inside the ornamental button frames and the menu, matching the business's button sample | A calligraphic, Garamond-style serif with an old-book feel, so it sits naturally between the hand-drawn script and the plain body font. |
| **Hanken Grotesk** (Google Fonts) | Everything else: descriptions, lists, the form's labels, fields and choices, and the "Send my intake" button | A quiet, clean typeface that stays easy to read at small sizes. |

Rules:
- **Brand font for headings only.** The Nyala Font has no numbers, hyphen, dashes, `&`, `/` or brackets, and its curls blur at small sizes. So anything with numbers, or anything that must be read quickly, uses Hanken Grotesk.
- **Anaktoria for the second level only.** It has one weight and a slanted, calligraphic hand, so it's kept to titles, prices, short intro lines and the framed buttons and menu links (at 17px and up), never form labels or fields. Two of its built-in extras are adjusted: its decorative swash word endings are switched off (at text size they looked like stray full stops), and the big date number uses its "lining" figures, which stand full height like capitals, instead of its default old-style ones.
- **One weight.** The brand font has a single weight, and the browser is told never to fake bold or light versions of it.
- **No labels above headings.** No small "eyebrow" labels sit above headings; the heading speaks for itself.

### Layout and shapes

- **Home page:** the plain paper fixed behind two sections that scroll over it; on wide screens the first screen shows the business's whole framed design.
- **Hero:** artwork, headline, introduction, and button form one centered vertical group. Desktop uses at least one viewport; mobile uses the content's height. The wordmark and menu remain above the group, and text is not absolutely positioned.
- **Readings:** content sits in a centred column 1320px wide.
- **Booking page:** one screen tall on desktop. The form sits in a bordered box up to 620px wide on the right, on the paper, with the glade and its heading on the left.
- **Buttons:** primary actions retain the business's ornamental frame from `Buttons/10.png`. Home-page navigation uses simple text links in the same Anaktoria font to give the primary action more emphasis. Reviews-page buttons and form controls retain their existing styles.
- **Shapes:** everything else the site draws is near-square: 3px corners on fields and chips, and 6px on panels. There are no pill shapes; the organic shapes come from the business's artwork: the paper's frame and the button frames; the service cards have rounded corners.
- **Depth:** soft, warm shadows under things laid on the paper (the service cards have a soft shadow under their rounded corners), never glowing outlines. Nyala has no shadow, like a print on the paper.

### Motion

| Element | Normal | Reduced motion |
|---|---|---|
| Home-page Nyala | Automatic 6-second float and subtle mouse tilt | Gentler automatic 8-second float, no tilt |
| Embossed symbols and colored cards | Static | Static |
| Booking-page Nyala | Gentle float every 4s | Smaller float every 5s |
| Scrolling into each section | Text is written in, letter by letter or word by word; frames, Vitruvian lines and engravings are drawn; buttons and scrolls drawn across; once each | The same (in-place reveals); the colored decks only fade |
| "Find Your Path" and "Readings" | The home page glides down to the readings | Jumps straight there |
| Cards and buttons | Lift or brighten on hover | Same, without the easing |

## Accessibility and reduced motion

- **Respecting motion settings:** some people turn off animations in their device settings (on Windows: *Settings → Accessibility → Visual effects → Animation effects*). The home-page Nyala uses a smaller, slower automatic float without cursor tilt when reduced motion is enabled. The booking figure uses its existing smaller float, as described in the motion table.
- **Keyboard:** everything can be reached with the keyboard. Focused items show a thin cream outline.
- **Screen readers:** decorative layers (the paper, Nyala, embossed symbols, and colored card fan) are hidden from them. The readings' wording is real text in the pricing columns (the engravings are decorative, with empty alt text); earlier, it lived in the service-card images, so each has a hidden heading with the reading's name and a text description of its formats. The form uses proper labels and groupings, announces errors, and connects each error message to its field.
- **Readability:** cream text on the rust paper reads clearly, helped by a soft warm shadow.
- **Headings:** each page has one main heading, with the section headings below it in order, so screen-reader users can jump between them.
- **Phones:** every section adapts to phone screens. On the first screen the bottom band stacks; in the readings the cards stack; on the booking page the glade sits above the form, and the fields and choices stack.

## Maintenance tools

These are only needed if the logo or one of the business's designs changes. They run in Windows PowerShell from the project folder. Converting images needs **ffmpeg** to be installed.

**If the logo changes:**
1. Make a new transparent cut-out and save it as `assets/nyala.png`, with any gaps inside the figure filled.
2. Rebuild it sharply:
   ```
   powershell -File tools\rebuild-nyala.ps1
   ffmpeg -i tools\nyala-rebuilt.png -c:v libwebp -lossless 1 -compression_level 6 assets\nyala.webp
   ```
3. Check the booking-page figure's fit. The home page uses the separate Vitruvian artwork; `js/main.js` is no longer loaded there.

Keep the WebP file **lossless**: normal WebP compression blurs the edges between her colours.

**If the paper design changes:** the selected source is `assets/Background/12.png`. Run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\build-paper.ps1` to rebuild both backgrounds and their provenance notes. To deliberately choose another file, pass `-Source 'assets/Background/filename.png'`. If its frame or label moves, update the framed hero positions in `css/style.css`. Update the `--paper` fallback color to match the selected paper.

**If the button frame changes:** save the new design over `Buttons/10.png` and run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\build-frame.ps1`. The script's crop and lettering-removal numbers fit the current file, so check them first if the new design is laid out differently.

**If the uploaded tarot artwork changes:** update the source mapping in `tools/build-deck-assets.ps1`, then run it to rebuild `assets/deck/`. Keep the originals and provenance notes. The fan and embossed placements are in the final artwork section of `css/style.css`.

**If a service card changes:** save the new design over its file in `ServicesCards/` (2000×2500 with a transparent surround, like the originals), then run:
```
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\build-cards.ps1
```
If a new design is a different size or shape, update the crop numbers in the script first, and the `width`/`height` on the card images in `index.html`.

**Where to change things:**

| To change… | Edit |
|---|---|
| A price | The `offer-price` lines in the readings section of `index.html` |
| A card's wording | The card's design in `ServicesCards/` (then rebuild, see above), plus its hidden heading and image description in `index.html` |
| Form questions or the areas-to-explore lists | `book.html` |
| Error messages | `MESSAGES` in `js/booking.js` |
| Where intake answers are sent | The `TODO` in `js/booking.js` |
| Nyala's float height or speed | `nyala-float` in `css/style.css` |

## How the design evolved

The current design is the result of several rounds of feedback. In brief:

1. **First draft:** a dark, starry "night sky" site inspired by three reference sites (Mindly, Polaris, Solvana), with all sections on one long page.
2. **Natural and green:** reworked into a bright, illustrated landscape with Nyala's logo on a hilltop, then made more saturated, with 3D-looking cartoon grass.
3. **Starting over:** a new reference led to a dark top-down meadow, with Nyala lying in the grass among glowing mushrooms, coins and rings.
4. **Simplifying:** the mushrooms, coins, rings and glows were removed, and the grass was replaced with a blurred forest photo.
5. **The painted forest:** the photo was replaced with an anime-style painted forest, closer to a storybook reference the business supplied. Its glow was softened.
6. **Focusing on the landing page:** the other sections were parked so the first screen could be perfected.
7. **Nyala:** she was enlarged, lowered, rebuilt to be sharp, lit to match the forest, and made to float.
8. **Details:** glowing butterflies, the business's own hand-drawn font, a horizontal wordmark, the moon-phase line removed, and the button renamed to "Find Your Path".
9. **New pages:** the Readings and Offerings page with vine-wrapped cards, and the intake form built from the business's spec. Tarot Reading and the 30-Minute Tarot Session were then merged onto one card.
10. **The booking layout:** the intake page went through a centred split card (which read as "a page inside a page") to the glade on the left and a bordered form box laid over the forest on the right. The box now scrolls inside itself, so the section is one screen tall with even margins above and below.
11. **One page, then the mix:** the three pages were first joined into one scrollable page over a single fixed forest. That was then settled as a mix: the first screen and the readings stay together on the home page, while the booking form went back to its own focused page, so its scrolling box doesn't compete with a scrolling page.
12. **The booking calendar and a second font:** a preferred-date calendar was added for every reading with tarot, then redesigned to read as a proper booking calendar. Anaktoria became the secondary font once a copy licensed "free for any use" (version 6.31) was supplied.
13. **The business's own cards:** the moss-green reading cards were replaced by the business's lace card designs, which also renamed the readings and changed how they're delivered (a PDF with an audio recording, or live). The wording across both pages was updated to match. The buttons and menu links then took on the business's ornamental frame, with labels in Anaktoria, and the thin corner brackets around the headline and the booking glade were removed.
14. **The business's paper:** the painted forest was replaced by the business's own rust paper with its embossed frame, which now frames the first screen with the wordmark in its label. The colours moved from moonlight and forest green to cream and deep rust-brown, Nyala went back to her own colours, and the butterflies and ivy, which belonged to the forest, were removed.
